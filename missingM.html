Why the Missing Middle Matters

"Bridge the gap, close the trap, help futures map a new path"

There’s a growing group of students who don’t qualify for NSFAS yet still can’t afford the costs of tertiary education.

They are the Missing Middle, hardworking and too often left behind.

The Missing Middle Fund bridges that gap by covering tuition and essential study costs for these students. With the help of our partners every contribution takes us closer to ensuring that financial status never determines who gets to graduate.

Your support helps keep dreams alive, one semester, one student, one story at a time.

How You're Making an Impact

Supporting Students Caught in the Gap

The Missing Middle Campaign assists students who earn too much to qualify for traditional funding, yet too little to afford university on their own—ensuring no talent is lost because of financial pressure.

Creating Life-Changing Opportunities

Your contribution provides deserving students with the means to study, graduate, and build a better future for themselves, their families, and their communities.

Strengthening South Africa’s Future Workforce

By helping the Missing Middle, you support students who will contribute to national growth, economic development, and the creation of a more skilled, empowered society.

A Gift That Multiplies Across Generations

Your generosity doesn’t just assist one student—it creates a ripple effect of opportunity, stability, and progress that benefits future generations of South Africans.
