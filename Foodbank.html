Every two weeks, students in need receive parcels packed with essentials from rice and canned food to soap and sanitary pads.

Support the Food Bank

Why the Food Bank Matters

"Hope we share, shows we care, lifting students here, today."

At UJ, no student should ever have to choose between a meal and a degree. Yet during high-pressure exam periods, hunger remains one of the most persistent challenges across our campuses.

Your contribution helps us stock shelves and restore dignity to students working toward a better future.

The UJ Food Bank exists to ensure that every student has access to nutritious meals when they need them most. Since 2022, more than 8 000 students have been supported through food parcels, vouchers, and on-campus drives, a lifeline made possible through donors like you.

Each contribution, regardless of size, creates a ripple effect that impacts not only individual students but the entire UJ community and society at large.

How You're Making an Impact

Fueling students for success

Over 8,000+ food parcels delivered to ensure students stay nourished and focused, especially during exam season when proper nutrition matters most.

R750 Feeds a Student

Just R750 supports one student, providing essential snacks and meals that help them concentrate, stay healthy, and perform academically.

Breaking Barriers Through Nutrition

Access to food should never be a barrier to education. Your support helps remove hunger as an obstacle, allowing students to thrive.

Community Giving, Real Impact

Every contribution transforms lives. Together, donors, staff, and partners are creating a stronger, healthier university community. One student at a time.
