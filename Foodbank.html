Why the Food Bank Matters

No Student Should Go Hungry

Central to UJ’s efforts is the UJ Food Bank Project, which provides meal assistance to students in need. This initiative offers well-balanced meal packs containing essential ingredients to support students’ health. The project relies on donations of non-perishable food items such as rice, pasta, maize meal, sugar, tinned goods, and soup powder. These contributions are distributed across all campuses to reach those most in need.
