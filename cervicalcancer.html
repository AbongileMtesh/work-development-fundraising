"Sing with pride, let voices glide, lift hearts far and wide."

The UJ Choir is more than a collection of voices, it’s a symbol of unity, culture, and excellence.

Representing UJ and South Africa on international stages, the choir continues to inspire through its performances.

Donations to the Choir Fund help cover travel, training, and performance costs, ensuring that our students’ voices are heard wherever excellence is celebrated.

Each contribution, regardless of size, creates a ripple effect that impacts the UJ choir at large.

How You're Making an Impact

Empowering Talent and Passion

The UJ Choir Initiative nurtures students’ musical talent, providing opportunities to perform, learn, and grow. Through training, mentorship, and rehearsals, choir members develop discipline, creativity, and confidence that extend beyond the stage.

Supporting Performances and Travel

Your contribution enables the choir to travel for national and international performances, participate in competitions, and share South African culture worldwide. Funding covers transport, accommodation, and logistical support for students to reach new audiences.

Enhancing Student Experience

Being part of the UJ Choir enriches students’ university experience by fostering teamwork, collaboration, and a sense of community. The initiative creates lifelong memories while developing leadership, communication, and performance skills.

Leaving a Lasting Cultural Legacy

By supporting the choir, donors invest in the preservation and promotion of musical excellence. Each performance reflects UJ’s commitment to arts, culture, and student empowerment, inspiring audiences and uplifting communities both locally and abroad.
