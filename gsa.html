GSA10: Transformative Futures

Over the past decade, the GSA has expanded access to students historically excluded by race, gender, and income, while rethinking what architecture is and who it serves.

Here, architecture is understood as a living practice - shaped by place, community, and the realities of African cities.

The Graduate School of Architecture at the University of Johannesburg was founded not just to teach architecture, but to transform it.
Donate Any Amount

Transformative Futures is our commitment to the next decade: building an architectural future that is innovative, inclusive, and grounded in transformation.

Your support helps expand opportunities for students, strengthens the tools and spaces needed for learning and research, and brings new voices, ideas, and collaborations into the school.

Architecture shapes how we live, gather, and belong. It influences whose histories are preserved and whose futures are imagined.

This future is being built now. Join us in making Transformative Futures possible.
