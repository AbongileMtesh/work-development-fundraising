Benefits of Giving

Tax Benefits & Compliance Benefits
UJ is a registered public benefit organisation, meaning your gift may qualify for:

  • ✅ Section 18A Tax Deduction (claimable on donations)
  • ✅ Section 11D R&D Incentives (for research investments)
  • ✅ B-BBEE Scorecard Points (boost your compliance rating)
  • ✅ Corporate CSI Benefits (enhance brand impact through giving)

Why Partner With UJ?
• Trusted by leading South African companies
• Transparent bursary and fund allocation
• High-impact, student-focused giving
🧡 Your gift goes exactly where you intend it to.
