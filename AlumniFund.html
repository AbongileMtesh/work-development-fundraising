Why the Annual Alumni Fund Matters

"You walked this campus, Help someone else keep walking."

UJ takes pride in the calibre of turce-alumni it has produced since its existence. These are people who continue to make positive motive contributions towards the development and transformation of the university.

Your support transforms lives on campus and beyond.

Since its inception, the Alumni Annual Fund has supported thousands of students through scholarships, emergency grants, and academic resources. Our alumni community continues to be the backbone of student success, providing not just financial assistance but mentorship and networking opportunities that extend far beyond graduation.

Each contribution, regardless of size, creates a ripple effect that impacts not only individual students but the entire UJ community and society at large.

How You're Making an Impact

Emergency Needs

Providing financial aid for urgent student needs during unexpected crises.

Student Success
