Why the Student Outstanding Debt Campaign Matters

Your gift unlocks a future that’s already earned.

UJ aims to raise R27 million, to support students who do not have the financial means to clear their debt and to enter the world of work. The issue of student debt has deprived many deserving students of the opportunity to re-register at UJ to continue with their studies despite having met the academic requirements. With support from the UJ community and the public, the University can assist around 20,000 students to settle their outstanding fees. The University has helped raised R916 million to accommodate qualifying students with their studies through its Missing Middle Fundraising Campaign since 2016.
