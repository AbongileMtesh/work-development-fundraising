Support the Student Outstanding Debt Campaign

Why the Student Outstanding Debts Fund Matters

"You walked this campus, Help someone else keep walking."

For many UJ students, success comes with a hidden barrier: outstanding fees that block access to transcripts, qualifications, or job opportunities.

The Student Outstanding Debts Fund exists to lift that weight. By clearing debts, we allow graduates to collect their certificates, pursue careers, and support their families.

It’s more than a donation; it’s the start of a ripple effect that lasts a lifetime.

No one should be held back.

How You're Making an Impact

Clearing Debt, Restoring Opportunity

Student debt prevents thousands of deserving students from re-registering and completing their studies—even when they meet academic requirements. Your support helps remove this barrier and restores their path to success.

Empowering 20,000 Students to Graduate

UJ aims to raise R27 million to help 20,000 students settle outstanding fees. With this support, students can continue their academic journeys and enter the world of work with dignity and confidence.

Building on a Proven Legacy of Support

Since 2016, UJ—through initiatives like the Missing Middle Campaign—has raised R916 million to support financially vulnerable students. This campaign continues that legacy, ensuring more students receive the help they need to succeed.

Breaking the Cycle of Financial Exclusion

Debt shouldn’t define a student’s future. By contributing, you help reduce financial stress, promote academic completion, and strengthen South Africa’s pool of skilled graduates who will contribute to society and the economy.
