Why Funding the UJ Choir Matters

The globally renowned UJ Choir continues to contribute positively to the University of Johannesburg’s profile by representing the musical excellence of the university and with its successful proven track record of enhancing student life and their university experience. Your contribution to the UJ Choir will allow the choir to continue creating enriching and memorable local and international opportunities for over 95 UJ choir members. Being part of the prestigious UJ Choir teaches choral excellence discipline, which has produced graduates who excel in their academic and career prospects. Under the leadership of conductor Renette Bouwer and her prodigy Sizwe Mondlane, the UJ choir has released five albums which has received global recognition. They are all available on multiple streaming platforms, such as Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, YouTube music and Deezer.
