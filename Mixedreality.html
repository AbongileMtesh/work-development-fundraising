Why the Mixed Reality Simulation Project Matters

"See to learn, teach in turn, let virtual worlds spark real earn."

Imagine future teachers training in classrooms that don’t yet exist. The Mixed Reality Simulation (MRS) project at UJ’s Faculty of Education is developing a uniquely South African version of this technology by blending digital avatars and real-time learning environments to prepare educators for real-world challenges.

Your support transforms lives on campus and beyond.

Funding helps establish this permanent platform, ensuring South Africa’s teachers are trained with world-class tools and locally relevant context.

Each contribution, regardless of size, creates a ripple effect that impacts not only individual students but the entire UJ community and society at large.

How You're Making an Impact

Transforming Teacher Training Through Immersive Simulation

Mixed Reality Simulation allows student teachers to practice essential teaching skills in safe, lifelike environments. By interacting with digital learner avatars, they gain confidence and competence before entering real classrooms.

Strengthening Core Teaching Practices

MRS helps student teachers master foundation skills such as effective questioning, lesson delivery, and classroom management. Research from 2022 and 2023 shows significant improvements in their confidence, self-reflection, and teaching performance.

Building Local Expertise and Innovation

UJ has trained its own simulation specialists through the Mursion™ platform—an important step that ensures sustainable, high-quality simulations. These specialists bring avatars to life in real time, enhancing engagement and realism.

Advancing Research and Expanding Impact

Supported by UJ’s investment, the project has grown rapidly—from 15 students in 2022 to full integration in 2023 Teaching Methodology and Practicum modules. Ongoing research will continue to refine and expand the use of MRS across teacher education programmes.
