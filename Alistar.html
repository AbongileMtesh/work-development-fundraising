Why the Alistar Mokoena Education Fund Matters

Hope is real when it’s funded.

The University of Johannesburg believes education is a powerful tool for advancing knowledge, innovation, and personal growth, yet many students face daunting challenges that hinder their academic dreams. The Alistair Mokoena Education Fund, established by Dr. Alistair Mokoena, stands as a beacon of hope, offering vital support to help these aspiring scholars succeed, embodying a strong commitment to academic excellence and improving education access in South Africa. Join us in making a difference—your support can help transform lives and build a brighter future
